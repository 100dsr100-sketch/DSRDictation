Microphone
Live dictation always uses your device’s default microphone — the browser’s speech engine doesn’t let an app pick a specific one. For best accuracy: use a headset or hold the phone 15–20 cm away, in a quiet room, and speak at a steady, natural pace. Choose your preferred input as the system default in Windows Sound settings or Android settings.

    About
    DSR Dictation v1a. Live dictation needs Chrome (desktop) or Chrome on Android and an internet connection — it uses Google’s speech engine, the most accurate option that’s free. Typing, spelling, grammar and read-aloud all work offline once installed.
