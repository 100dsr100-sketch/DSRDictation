⚙ Settings

Dictation
Runs entirely on your device — nothing is sent to a server. The first time you use a model it downloads once (then works offline). Transcribes in short bursts as you pause, so it isn’t word-by-word live. Needs a reasonably powerful phone/PC.
Full list in ? commands above the note. Say “scratch that” to undo the last bit, “stop dictation” to pause.
Off = always add new text to the end of the note.
Microphone
Live dictation always uses your device’s default microphone — the browser’s speech engine doesn’t let an app pick a specific one. For best accuracy: use a headset or hold the phone 15–20 cm away, in a quiet room, and speak at a steady, natural pace. Set your preferred input as the system default in Windows Sound settings or Android settings.

    About
    DSR Dictation v1j. Live dictation needs Chrome (desktop) or Chrome on Android and an internet connection — it uses Google’s speech engine, the most accurate option that’s free. Typing, spelling, grammar, notes and read-aloud all work offline once installed.
