Dictation
Runs entirely on your device — your voice never leaves it. Moonshine shows your words while you talk and takes about a second per phrase on a phone; Whisper writes each phrase after you pause and is several times slower.
Full list in ? commands above the note. Say “scratch that” to undo the last bit, “stop dictation” to pause.

Saved with the note on this device (Notes → Recordings) so you can listen back. Works with the Private engine, and with Google on a PC (Android lets only Google’s engine use the mic).
My words
Say a short phrase, get any text — your email address, a sign-off, or the right spelling of a name the engine keeps getting wrong. Type \n for a new line.
Microphone
Live dictation always uses your device’s default microphone — the browser’s speech engine doesn’t let an app pick a specific one. For best accuracy: use a headset or hold the phone 15–20 cm away, in a quiet room, and speak at a steady, natural pace. Set your preferred input as the system default in Windows Sound settings or Android settings.
